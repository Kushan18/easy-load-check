About LoadCheck

LoadCheck is a next-generation Transport Compliance-Assist System designed to streamline highway logistics while ensuring safety and regulatory adherence.

Our Mission

To reduce transport delays and improve road safety by providing a transparent, efficient mechanism for load verification. We believe that compliance shouldn't be a bottleneck, but a guarantee of safety.

How It Works

  • 1. Transporters create a "Trip Bundle" with all vehicle, driver, and cargo details.
  • 2. Our Risk Engine calculates a safety score (Green/Yellow/Red) based on load capacity.
  • 3. A secure QR code is generated for the trip.
  • 4. Checkpost officers scan the QR to verify details instantly. Authorized trips get a "State Skip" flag to bypass further checks within the same state.
